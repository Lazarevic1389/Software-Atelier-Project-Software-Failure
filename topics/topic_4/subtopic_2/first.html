Cerner Millennium EHR Outages (2020–2022)

Context

Cerner is one of the world's largest suppliers of Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems. Its "Millennium" platform experienced multiple significant outages affecting hospitals, especially during the COVID-19 pandemic.

Cause

Software bugs and system integration failures following updates or under high load, which disrupted the central system used for patient records, medication orders, and test results.

Impact

The outages caused severe disruption to patient care coordination. Doctors and nurses lost access to vital patient data, leading to delays in treatment, medication errors, and increased risk during a public health crisis.
