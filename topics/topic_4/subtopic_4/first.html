UK NHS WannaCry Ransomware Attack (2017)

Context

A global cyberattack using WannaCry ransomware severely disrupted the UK's National Health Service (NHS), crippling hospitals and clinics.

Cause

The attack exploited a known Windows vulnerability called "EternalBlue." Many NHS computers were running outdated, unsupported operating systems (like Windows XP) and had not installed the available security patch.

Impact

Widespread cancellation of appointments and surgeries; ambulances were diverted; medical staff lost access to patient records. This led to significant treatment delays and posed a direct risk to patient safety.
